=============
Food delivery
=============

Connect online food delivery platforms to Odoo Point of Sale to receive orders, sync menus, and
manage store availability from a single interface.

.. cards::

   .. card:: UrbanPiper
      :target: food_delivery/urban_piper

      Use UrbanPiper to consolidate orders from multiple delivery platforms (such as Uber Eats,
      DoorDash, and Zomato).

   .. card:: Direct platform integrations
      :target: food_delivery/direct_platform_integrations

      Connect directly to GoFood and GrabFood without a third-party aggregator to manage menus,
      service hours, and incoming orders.
