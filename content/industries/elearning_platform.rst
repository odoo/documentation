==================
eLearning platform
==================

The **eLearning Platform** industry package helps online training businesses manage course content,
assessments, learner progress, course sales, and learner communication in Odoo. It connects the
course catalog with enrollment and continued learning.

Course teams can publish videos, articles, and resources, define assessments, and follow learner
progress. Paid courses use the online store, while configured free courses can support internal
training. Marketing campaigns and community discussions help learners discover courses and continue
learning after their first enrollment.

.. _industries/elearning_platform/basics:

Basics
======

The main dashboard provides access to every installed app in the database.

* The **eLearning** app: Organize course content, assessments, learner progress, and learning
  rewards.
* The **Website** app and the **eCommerce** app: Present the course catalog and sell access to paid
  courses.
* The **Email Marketing** app: Send course announcements and targeted learner campaigns.
* The **Social Marketing** app: Promote course content through the configured social channels.

.. _industries/elearning_platform/create-and-publish-courses:

Create and publish courses
==========================

1. Create a course in the **eLearning** app and complete its description and access settings.
2. Add lesson content, such as videos, articles, and downloadable resources.
3. Organize the lessons into a progression that suits the course.
4. Add quizzes or certification assessments and configure their requirements.
5. Review the course's website page and publish the content intended for learners.

Configure experience points, badges, or leaderboards when those rewards support the learning
experience. A certification depends on the configured assessment requirements; course completion
alone does not replace them.

:doc:`ELearning Documentation <../applications/websites/elearning>`

.. _industries/elearning_platform/sell-course-access:

Sell course access
==================

Use the **eCommerce** app to present paid courses in the online catalog. Complete the product
information, prices, and checkout payment methods. A confirmed purchase grants course access through
the configured course-product link.

Use price lists, discounts, gift offers, or course packages when the business offers promotions or
bundles. Configure free access for internal staff training where appropriate, and keep each course's
access settings aligned with its audience.

:doc:`Configure online course products <../applications/websites/ecommerce/configuration/products>`

.. _industries/elearning_platform/follow-learning-and-sales:

Follow learning and sales
=========================

Review learner progress and lesson completion in the **eLearning** app to identify where
participants stop or need further support. Use reviews, ratings, and comments to collect course
feedback.

Review sales reports for course revenue and popularity. Compare purchase activity with learner
progress when deciding which courses to update or develop next.

.. _industries/elearning_platform/promote-the-course-catalog:

Promote the course catalog
==========================

Share course previews, lesson excerpts, and announcements through the configured the **Social
Marketing** app accounts. Use the **Email Marketing** app for audiences based on interests or
previous course purchases.

Prepare reusable campaign templates and schedule messages around course launches or enrollment
periods. Review opens, clicks, and the associated sales activity to follow campaign results.

:doc:`Create and schedule social posts <../applications/marketing/social_marketing/social_posts>`

:doc:`Review email campaign results <../applications/marketing/email_marketing/analyze_metrics>`

.. _industries/elearning_platform/support-a-learner-community:

Support a learner community
===========================

Use course reviews and the website forum to encourage questions, discussion, and peer support.
Follow recurring questions when updating lessons or preparing additional learning resources.

For scheduled reminders, certification messages, or suggestions for a next course, add The
**Marketing Automation** app and configure the relevant audience and triggers.

.. tip::
   Review lessons with low completion rates, keep assessments aligned with the material, and
   use learning rewards where they serve the course. Reuse lesson excerpts in campaigns and
   group related courses when a bundle helps learners choose a learning path.

Additional apps
===============

Course development, learner feedback, shared resources, and enrollment follow-up can support the
platform:

* Use the **Knowledge** app and the **Documents** app to maintain course resources and working
  materials for the content team.
* Use the **Accounting** app to reconcile course payments and follow training revenue.
* Use the **Surveys** app to collect structured learner feedback.
* Use the **Project** app to plan course production and updates.
* Use the **Dashboards** app to bring learning and sales indicators into reporting views.
* Use the **Marketing Automation** app to organize reminders and course suggestions through
  configured campaigns.
