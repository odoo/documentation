=======
Bowling
=======

Welcome to the **Odoo Bowling package**!

A bowling venue brings lane bookings, walk-in customers, shoe rentals, and food orders together
throughout the day. This package connects the booking website and reception desk so staff can
follow lane availability and serve customers from one system.

Customers can reserve and pay for a session online, while staff manage bookings and sell lane
time, refreshments, and rental shoes through Point of Sale. Booking resources define the lanes
and their capacity, and the website provides a place for groups or businesses to ask about
events. The same setup can support everyday sessions and larger gatherings at the venue.

.. _industries/bowling/basics:

Basics
======

The main dashboard provides access to every installed app in the database.

* **Website** app: Present the venue and collect inquiries about parties and business events.
* **Appointments** app: Accept prepaid lane bookings for different session lengths.
* **Calendar** app: Review scheduled bookings.
* **Point of Sale** app: Sell walk-in sessions, refreshments, and shoe rentals at the venue.







.. _industries/bowling/configure-lane-bookings:

Configure lane bookings
=======================

Use **Appointments** to offer lane sessions on the website. Link the appointment type to the lane
resources and define the maximum number of players for each lane.

Create booking options for different session lengths while using the same resources. This keeps
the availability of each lane connected across the booking options. Configure online prepayment
for customers to pay when reserving their session.

Review booked sessions in **Calendar** when planning the day's lane use.

.. _industries/bowling/sell-sessions-and-refreshments:

Sell sessions and refreshments
==============================

Open **Point of Sale** to sell walk-in sessions, food, drinks, and rental shoes. Link food orders
to the customer's lane to help staff deliver them to the right group.

Sell shoe rentals and verify if they have been returned before closing a customer's tab.

.. _industries/bowling/self-service-ordering:

Self-service ordering
---------------------

Enable the self-ordering kiosk in the point-of-sale settings to let customers place their own
orders. Include the products available for self-service in the kiosk's catalog.

.. _industries/bowling/present-the-venue-online:

Present the venue online
========================

Use **Website** to publish the venue's booking options and practical information. A contact form
can collect inquiries about business events and other group bookings.

Keep the website's branding and booking reminders consistent with the venue. Use training
resources such as :doc:`Odoo eLearning <../applications/websites/elearning>`
when preparing staff to use the booking and sales flows.
