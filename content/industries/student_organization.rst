.. _industries/student_organization:

====================
Student organization
====================

The **Student Organization** industry package helps student organizations manage memberships,
events, registrations, merchandise, volunteer tasks, and activity costs in Odoo. It connects
committee work with the shared information needed to continue projects and prepare the next
handover.

Use the package to publish the organization's news, sell event tickets, and assign preparation
tasks. Shared contact, expense, and project records help the next committee continue the work with
the information it needs.

.. _industries/student_organization/basics:

Basics
======

The main dashboard provides access to every installed app in the database.

* The **Members** app: Follow membership records, active periods, and dues.
* The **Events** app: Manage student-event registrations, tickets, and attendance.
* The **Website** app and the **eCommerce** app: Present the organization and sell memberships and
  merchandise online.
* The **Point of Sale** app: Take membership, ticket, and product payments at booths or events.
* The **Sales** app: Prepare offers and apply the configured member pricelists.
* The **Project** app: Organize initiatives with tasks and deadlines.
* The **Purchase** app: Order merchandise, event supplies, and services.
* The **Expenses** app: Record committee and volunteer expenses for approval.
* The **Accounting** app: Follow fees, event revenue, expenses, and the organization’s budget.
* The **Email Marketing** app and the **Social Marketing** app: Share news and upcoming activities
  with selected audiences.

.. note::
   The Odoo mobile app provides access to the database from a phone.

Use a separate `Odoo trial database <http://Odoo.com/trial>`__ for the sample organization
workflows.

.. _industries/student_organization/sell-and-follow-memberships:

Sell and follow memberships
===========================

Create membership products with the price and membership period offered by the organization.
Configure the associated member level and pricelist so a qualifying purchase applies the intended
benefits to the contact.

Publish membership products in the **eCommerce** app for online purchase. For a booth or event sale,
open the **Point of Sale** app, select the customer, add the membership product, and take payment.
Follow the resulting membership record in :guilabel:`Members`.

Set the member prices for merchandise or events in the relevant pricelist. Review active periods and
renewals; the package's benefits follow the membership status and its configured period.

With the **Subscriptions** app, a recurring membership product and recurring price can be used for
periodic billing instead of preparing each period's sale separately.

.. _industries/student_organization/organize-events-and-ticket-sales:

Organize events and ticket sales
================================

Create an event in the **Events** app for a gala, workshop, talk, or other activity. Set the date,
venue, capacity, ticket types, and prices. Prepare the website event page and publish its
registration options.

Sell tickets online or through the configured event-ticket products in the **Point of Sale** app.
Record the attendee information and follow registrations and arrival through Events. Configure
confirmation messages and reminders for the event.

.. _industries/student_organization/coordinate-preparation-tasks:

Coordinate preparation tasks
============================

Create a project for the initiative and add tasks for logistics, promotion, purchases, and setup.
Assign committee members or volunteers, set deadlines, and update task status as work progresses.

Use the Kanban or Gantt view to follow responsibilities and timing. Review the project dashboard for
the related budget, expenses, and financial records configured for that project.

.. _industries/student_organization/purchase-supplies-and-record-expenses:

Purchase supplies and record expenses
=====================================

In the **Purchase** app, request vendor quotes for merchandise, event equipment, or other supplies
and services. Review prices and dates, confirm the agreed orders, and follow receipt of goods or
completion of the service.

Committee members and volunteers record expenses in the **Expenses** app with their receipts. Submit
them for the configured approval and reimbursement workflow. Link campaign or event costs to the
relevant analytic account when project cost tracking is used.

.. _industries/student_organization/publish-news-and-sell-merchandise:

Publish news and sell merchandise
=================================

Use the **Website** app for the organization's mission, news, event links, and participation
information. Publish merchandise, event kits, and other products through the online shop with their
prices and images.

Prepare newsletters in the **Email Marketing** app for the selected member and supporter audience.
Follow opens and clicks in the mailing report. Connect supported accounts in the **Social
Marketing** app to schedule posts about events, elections, fundraising, and new merchandise.

Additional apps
===============

Committee handovers, event staffing, and recurring membership billing can be supported alongside
student activities:

* Use the **Documents** app to organize meeting minutes, budgets, and committee reports.
* Use the **Knowledge** app to maintain onboarding guides, policies, and handover instructions.
* Use the **Planning** app to assign volunteer shifts at events and booths.
* Use the **Subscriptions** app to manage periodic membership fees.
* Use the **Dashboards** app to review memberships, ticket sales, merchandise, and campaign results.
