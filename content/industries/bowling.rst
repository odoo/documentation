=======
Bowling
=======

A bowling venue brings :ref:`lane bookings <industries/bowling/configure-lane-bookings>`, walk-in
customers, shoe rentals, and food orders together throughout the day. This package connects the
booking website and reception desk so staff can follow lane availability and serve customers from
one system.

Customers can reserve and pay for a session online, while staff manage bookings and sell lane
time, refreshments, and rental shoes through the **Point of Sale** app. Booking resources define
the lanes and their capacity, and the website provides a place for groups or businesses to ask
about events. The same setup can support everyday sessions and larger gatherings at the venue.

.. _industries/bowling/basics:

Basics
======

The main dashboard provides access to every installed app in the database.

* The **Website** app: Present the venue and collect inquiries about parties and business events.
* The **Appointments** app: Accept prepaid lane bookings for different session lengths.
* The **Calendar** app: Review scheduled bookings.
* The **Point of Sale** app: Sell walk-in sessions, refreshments, and shoe rentals at the venue.

.. _industries/bowling/configure-lane-bookings:

Configure lane bookings
======================

Use the **Appointments** app to offer lane sessions on the website. Link the appointment type to the
lane resources and define the maximum number of players for each lane.

Create booking options for different session lengths while using the same resources. This keeps
the availability of each lane connected across the booking options. Configure online prepayment
for customers to pay when reserving their session.

Review booked sessions in the **Appointments** app's Gantt or Calendar view when planning the day's
lane use.

.. _industries/bowling/sell-sessions-and-refreshments:

Sell sessions and refreshments
==============================

Open the **Point of Sale** app to sell walk-in sessions, food, drinks, and rental shoes. Link food
orders to the customer's lane to help staff deliver them to the right group.

Sell shoe rentals and verify if they have been returned before closing a customer's tab.

.. _industries/bowling/self-service-ordering:

Self-service ordering
---------------------

Enable the self-ordering kiosk in the point-of-sale settings to let customers place their own
orders. Include the products available for self-service in the kiosk's catalog.

.. _industries/bowling/present-the-venue-online:

Present the venue online
========================

Use the **Website** app to publish the venue's booking options and practical information. A contact
form can collect inquiries about business events and other group bookings.

Keep the website's branding and booking reminders consistent with the venue.

.. _industries/bowling/additional-apps:

Additional apps
===============

Group events, customer campaigns, and staff administration can complement lane bookings:

* Use the **Social Marketing** and **Email Marketing** apps to announce special sessions, offers, and
  events.
* Use the **Employees** and **Time Off** apps to manage staff records and leave requests.
* Use the **Expenses** app to record staff expenses related to venue operations.
* Use the **Accounting** app to reconcile venue payments and review financial reporting.
