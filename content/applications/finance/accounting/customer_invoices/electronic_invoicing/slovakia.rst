=========================
Odoo electronic invoicing
=========================

Odoo Invoicing is your trusted partner for safe, efficient, and legally compliant e-invoicing
solutions tailored to Slovakia's regulatory standards, and compatible with the European `Peppol
<https://peppol.org/about/>`_ framework.

Legal framework for e-Invoicing in Slovakia
===========================================

In Slovakia, e-Invoicing is governed by the `VAT Act No. 222/2004 Coll. <https://www.mfsr.sk/en/taxes-customs-accounting/indirect-taxes/vat/act-222/2004-coll-on-value-added-tax/>`_,
as amended by Act No. 385/2025 Coll.. The amendment was approved by the National Council in December
2025 and aligns with the EU’s `VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) Directive 2025/516 <https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en>`_
and `EU Directive 2014/55/EU <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L_202500516>`_.

From 1 January 2027, VAT payers established in Slovakia must issue structured electronic invoices
compliant with the European standard `EN 16931 <https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/spaces/DIGITAL/pages/467108950/EN+16931+compliance>`_
for domestic B2B and B2G transactions. All businesses and legal entities in Slovakia, including
those not registered for VAT, must be able to receive them. Invoices are exchanged via the Peppol
networkthrough certified delivery service providers (“Digital postmen”) accredited by the Slovak
`Financial Administration <https://www.financnasprava.sk/en/financial-administration>`_, which also
acts as the Peppol Authority for Slovakia. In addition, invoice data is reported to the Slovak
Financial Administration in near real time. For public procurement, the Peppol-based infrastructure
replaces the former IS EFA system.

Compliance with Slovak e-invoicing regulations
==============================================

Odoo Invoicing makes it easy for businesses to send, receive, and report their invoices. Here is how
Odoo ensures compliance:

- Peppol-compliant formats: Odoo supports the Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (UBL) format, based on EN
  16931, and registers Slovak companies under their :abbr:`DIČ (Tax Identification Number)`, as
  required by the Slovak Peppol Authority.
- Certified delivery service: Odoo acts as your Peppol access point, so you can send and receive
  e-invoices directly from your database, for both B2B and B2G transactions.
- Automatic VAT calculation: Odoo automatically applies Slovak VAT rates (23%, 19%, and 5%) and
  ensures that each invoice includes the legally required information.
- Automatic tax reporting: The invoice data is reported to the Slovak Financial Administration for
  each invoice sent or received via Peppol. The resulting tax data document is automatically
  attached to the related invoice or bill.
- Secure storage and retrieval: In accordance with Slovak requirements to store invoices for ten
  years, Odoo securely archives all invoices and their tax data documents, so they can be retrieved
  during audits.
